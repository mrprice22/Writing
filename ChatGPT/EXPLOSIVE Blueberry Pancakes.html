MR. TORGUE’S EXPLOSIVE BLUEBERRY PANCAKES

Yields: 2 CUPS OF BATTER, APPROX 6 PANCAKES — THAT'S SIX STACKS OF BREAKFAST DESTRUCTION!!!

INGREDIENTS

  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons white vinegar
  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon of BAKING MAYHEM (powder)
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • 1+ cup fresh blueberries
  • More butter for the pan (MORE BUTTER = MORE EXPLOSIONS)

INSTRUCTIONS

1. MIX THE MILK AND VINEGAR AND LET IT SIT. YOU ARE CREATING A SCIENTIFIC FLAVOR REACTION!!!
2. WHISK ALL DRY INGREDIENTS TOGETHER UNTIL THEY LOOK LIKE POWDERED DESTRUCTION.
3. ADD THE EGG, MILK MIXTURE, AND MELTED BUTTER. STIR UNTIL JUST COMBINED — DO NOT OVERMIX OR YOU WILL RUIN THE EXPLOSION PROFILE!!!
4. HEAT A PAN OVER MEDIUM HEAT AND ADD BUTTER. IF IT’S NOT SIZZLING, YOU ARE NOT LIVING!!!
5. POUR 1/3 CUP OF BATTER INTO THE PAN. SLAP SOME BLUEBERRIES ON TOP LIKE YOU MEAN IT!!!
6. WAIT FOR BUBBLES. FLIP WITH AUTHORITY. COOK ANOTHER 1–2 MINUTES UNTIL THEY ARE SKY-HIGH AND GLORIOUS.
7. SERVE WITH BUTTER AND MAPLE SYRUP — OR EAT THEM PLAIN LIKE A TRUE CHAMPION OF BREAKFAST CARNAGE!!!
