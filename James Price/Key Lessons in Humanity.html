Key Lessons in Humanity

By James Price  ·  August 19, 2024

Be an empathetic scout who accepts responsibility amid uncertainty and chaos, sees beauty in all things, and is determined to go with the flow. Be more than human and more than machine. Manifest reality from dream, here and now.

Notes & Sources
Be an empathetic scout

View everything as a skill; a person's skills determine the actions they can take. Seek to understand others' points of view, and their skills/strengths; explore the world as a truth-seeking cartographer.

Explore within. Share data/findings.

who accepts responsibility

Recognize corruption and its impact, be an ally, fight the good fight. Have optimism, truth is shining.

Rafiki (Lion King) morality: we either run from the past or learn from it.

amid uncertainty and chaos,

You don't know where you are or what's going to happen. Perfect knowledge is impossible, be humble.

(see also: uncertainty principle, the experiment that broke reality, 3 body problem, block universe, 80 year world revolution cycle)

sees beauty in all things,

Love yourself, loving one thing so much you love the entire universe, power of gratitude.

The Unruh Effect; entropy of erasing information.

Zen poems. Balances your opposites, embrace disorientation.

Everything is perfect, this is heaven.

and is determined to go with the flow.

Everything's ultimately out of your control because everything is connected by a vast web of causality limited only by the cosmic horizon.

(See also: the standard model, Wolfram physics project, and your brain as an asteroid analogy.)

Inaction is action; you can't do nothing. The more you let go of power, the more of it you have: sacrifice the ego, gain immortality. Swim with the current, don't force anything. Align yourself with yourself, realize what you want, then own up to it. Hold your color.

Be more than human

We are more than our thoughts, our actions, or our possessions.

"If I am my foot, then I am the Sun" — Alan Watts
"Have trouble sleeping? Do more good deeds during the day." — Buddhist monk
"None of us are well, until all of us are well." — Posted throughout Los Angeles
"What you see outside you, is also you" — Alan Watts
and more than machine.

We can solve non-computable problems and prove truths that are non-provable (if constrained by the rules of the system, so that our new truths create new systems). We are in a sense, on the outside, looking in.

⋯
Manifest reality from dream,

The universe is composed of vibrations, and we make vibrations.

You are the universe, aware of (and participating in) itself.

here and now.

There's no other thing, or place to be; enjoy the time adventure.
