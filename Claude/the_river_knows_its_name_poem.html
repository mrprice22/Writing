The River Knows Its Name

We did not choose the river's start,

the banks that shaped us, stone by stone,

yet here we flow with something like a heart —

the universe aware, and not alone.

The self is just a name for where

the water gathers, briefly bright.

No ghost commands the current there,

no pilot steers the wave toward light.

Every choice was always going to be.

Every wound a lesson, not a debt.

The hand that struck you — also not quite free —

was shaped by storms it couldn't yet forget.

So where does blame dissolve? Right here:

where cause meets cause in endless chain,

where resentment softens into clear-

eyed understanding of another's pain.

No mistakes. Only what occurred.

Only the lesson that the outcome holds.

Shame locks the door; let it be stirred

open — the examined life unfolds.

The wave believes itself apart.

The ocean knows the wave is home.

You are the universe's beating heart,

briefly beautiful, and never alone.

What happened always will have been —

permanent the way all moments are.

The river flows through everything unseen,

and you, dear cause, are also a star.

After The Determinist Manifesto
